# John F. Kennedy

John Fitzgerald Kennedy (May 29, 1917 – November 22, 1963), often referred to by his initials JFK, was an American politician who served as the 35th president of the United States from 1961 until his assassination in 1963.

During his presidency, Kennedy initiated the creation of the Peace Corps, pushed for the Civil Rights Act of 1964, and heavily supported the Space Race. In a defining speech to Congress in 1961, and later at Rice University in 1962, he committed the United States to the goal of landing a man on the Moon and returning him safely to Earth by the end of the decade, a goal that would eventually be realized by the Apollo 11 mission.

His presidency also saw high tensions with communist states in the Cold War. The Cuban Missile Crisis in October 1962 is widely considered the closest the world has come to nuclear war.
